Dogs and Science Award for Excellence in Science Communication

Presented by Dogs and Science at the Canine Science Forum 2027

About the Award

Scientific discoveries can make a difference in the world when they are shared, understood, and meaningfully connected to society. Effective science communication plays a vital role in making research accessible, encouraging critical thinking, fostering dialogue, and supporting evidence-informed decisions. Yet the time, creativity, and expertise invested in communicating science often receive far less recognition than traditional academic achievements.

To celebrate and encourage these efforts, Dogs and Science is delighted to introduce the Award for Excellence in Science Communication at the Canine Science Forum 2027.

The award recognises outstanding initiatives that communicate scientific knowledge about dogs and other canids beyond academic audiences, strengthening public understanding, engagement with science, and dialogue between researchers, professionals, and society.

One outstanding initiative will be selected for the award at CSF 2027.

What Does the Award Include?

The award recipient will receive:

  • Featured presentation at CSF 2027:

    An invitation to present the awarded initiative in a dedicated 15-minute conference presentation, sharing its objectives, achievements, challenges, and lessons learned.

  • Complimentary conference registration:

    Full coverage of the registration fee for the Canine Science Forum 2027.

  • Science communication training voucher:

    A voucher for an online science communication course offered by NaWik (National Institute for Science Communication).

  • Award certificate and recognition:

    Official recognition through the Canine Science Forum website and Dogs and Science communication channels.

Conditions

Eligibility Criteria

AWho can apply

The award is open to individuals of any nationality, professional background, and career stage, including researchers, PhD students, veterinarians, animal behaviour and welfare professionals, science communicators, and educators.

Applicants do not need to hold an academic position or possess a specific academic degree.

The award recognises a specific science communication project or initiative related to dogs or other canids.

BFormats

Eligible initiatives may include:

  • Public lectures, workshops, webinars, and educational events
  • Podcasts, videos, documentaries, and social media initiatives
  • Popular science articles, books, blogs, and educational resources
  • Participatory projects involving dog owners, professionals, or the wider public
  • Interactive exhibitions, digital tools, and innovative communication formats
  • Initiatives translating canine research into accessible, evidence-informed practice

Initiatives may target broad public audiences or specific groups, such as dog owners, animal care professionals, educators, or policymakers.

CRequirements

To be eligible, initiatives must:

  • Be grounded in scientific evidence and demonstrate a commitment to accuracy, transparency, and responsible communication.
  • Have been implemented, at least in part, with sufficient material or outcomes available for evaluation.
  • Demonstrate a substantial contribution by the applicant to the initiative's development or implementation.

Both individual and collaborative projects are welcome. For collaborative initiatives, one individual must be nominated as the award candidate and conference presenter, with appropriate acknowledgement of collaborators.

Self-nominations and nominations by others are welcome. Individuals nominated by others must consent to their nomination.

Application

How to Apply

Applicants or nominators must complete the official Dogs and Science Award for Excellence in Science Communication application form.

Applications must include:

  1. 1

    Applicant Information

    Name, affiliation or professional role (if applicable), country, and contact details. For nominations by others, the nominator's details must also be provided.

  2. 2

    Description of the Initiative (maximum 400 words)

    The description should address:

    Background and objectives:
    What motivated the initiative, and what does it aim to achieve?
    Target audience:
    Who is the initiative intended for?
    Communication approach:
    Which formats or strategies were used to engage the audience?
    Scientific foundation:
    How is the communication informed by scientific research, and how is scientific accuracy ensured?
    Outcomes and impact:
    What has been achieved, and what evidence of engagement, learning, or impact is available?
    Lessons learned:
    What insights or experiences could benefit others communicating canine science?
  3. 3

    Supporting Materials

    Applicants may provide up to three examples or links demonstrating their work, such as videos, podcasts, articles, websites, educational materials, or audience feedback.

  4. 4

    Applicant's Contribution

    A brief description of the applicant's role in developing or implementing the initiative, particularly for collaborative projects.

Assessment

Selection and Evaluation

Applications will be evaluated by a selection committee with expertise in canine science and science communication, based on the following criteria:

Scientific accuracy and integrity:
Reliability, appropriate representation of scientific evidence, transparency about uncertainty, and responsible communication
Clarity and accessibility:
Effectiveness in making scientific knowledge understandable, relevant, and accessible to the intended audience
Creativity and originality:
Innovative or particularly effective communication approaches
Audience engagement and impact:
Evidence of meaningful engagement, learning, dialogue, or other outcomes relevant to the initiative's objectives
Contribution to public understanding:
Potential to strengthen understanding, critical thinking, and evidence-informed perspectives on dogs and other canids

The committee will consider each initiative's objectives, target audience, scope, and available resources.

Reach alone will not be considered equivalent to impact. Small-scale initiatives demonstrating meaningful outcomes may be equally valuable as large-scale communication campaigns.

Formal impact evaluations are not required, but applicants are encouraged to provide evidence of outcomes where available.

Presentation

Award Presentation and Notification

  • The selected recipient will be invited to present their initiative at CSF 2027, highlighting its communication approach, achievements, challenges, and lessons learned. The presentation aims to inspire other researchers and professionals to engage in effective science communication.

  • The notification date will be announced. The award recipient will subsequently be announced on the Canine Science Forum 2027 website and through Dogs and Science communication channels.

About Dogs and Science

Dogs and Science (www.dogsandscience.com) is dedicated to making scientific knowledge about dogs accessible, understandable, and relevant beyond academia. Through science communication, education, and collaboration, Dogs and Science aims to strengthen connections between research, professional practice, and society.

The Dogs and Science Award for Excellence in Science Communication celebrates individuals who contribute to this mission and promotes greater recognition of science communication as an essential part of scientific practice.
