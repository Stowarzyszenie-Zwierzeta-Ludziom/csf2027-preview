Early Career Scientist Award – Canine Science Forum 2027

Supporting and celebrating the next generation of researchers has always been an important tradition of the Canine Science Forum.

Following this tradition, the Organizing Committee of the Canine Science Forum 2027 is delighted to invite outstanding early career scientists, including current PhD students, to apply for the Early Career Scientist Award.

The award recognises promising researchers who have demonstrated scientific excellence, originality, and the potential to make significant contributions to the field of canine science.

At a glance

The Award

One outstanding candidate will be selected to deliver a plenary lecture at the Canine Science Forum 2027, taking place in Warsaw, Poland, from 14–17 June 2027.

The successful candidate will receive:

  • Coverage of travel expenses (up to 1000 EUR)
  • Full coverage of accommodation expenses
  • Free registration to the conference
Who can apply

Eligibility Criteria

Applications are welcome from researchers of any nationality who meet the following criteria.

  1. 1

    Career Stage

    Applicants must be either:

    • Current PhD students (or students pursuing an equivalent research doctorate), or
    • Early career researchers within seven years of completing their PhD (or equivalent higher research degree).

    Applicants who have taken maternity or paternity leave to care for a newborn child, or who have undertaken military service, may request an extension of the seven-year eligibility period in accordance with the award regulations.

  2. 2

    Academic Position

    Applicants must not hold a permanent academic or equivalent research position.

  3. 3

    Scientific Track Record

    Applicants must demonstrate a promising scientific track record appropriate to their career stage, reflecting their research achievements, contributions, and potential for future scientific excellence.

  4. 4

    Research Contribution

    Applicants must have made a substantial intellectual contribution to the research project forming the basis of their proposed lecture.

    Postdoctoral researchers

    For postdoctoral researchers, this would normally involve acting as a principal investigator or co-principal investigator.

    PhD students

    For PhD students, the proposed research may form part of their doctoral project conducted under academic supervision. Applicants must demonstrate a significant and clearly identifiable role in the conception, development, or execution of the research.

  5. 5

    Research Relevance

    The proposed presentation must be relevant to one or more of the following major themes related to dogs and other canids:

    • Cognition
    • Behavioural biology
    • Evolution of canines
    • Genetics and endo-/phenotyping
    • Dogs and humans
    • Applied aspects of canine biology
Plenary

Award Lecture

The selected candidate will be invited to deliver a plenary lecture reviewing the proposed research topic, including the scientific background, research questions, methodological approaches, and relevant findings from their own work.

The lecture should highlight the originality, scientific significance, and broader implications of the research.

Important

The proposed lecture must be based on research that has already generated substantive findings. Proposals describing exclusively planned research without any results are not eligible.

Application

How to Apply

Applicants must complete the official Early Career Scientist Award application form and submit it together with a brief curriculum vitae.

The application must include:

  • A completed application form
  • A brief curriculum vitae, including relevant academic achievements and research experience
  • A list of the applicant's five most relevant scientific publications, where available

Applicants who have fewer than five publications, particularly current PhD students, may submit all publications available to date. A minimum number of publications is not required.

Assessment

Selection and Evaluation

Applications will be evaluated by the international Scientific Committee of the Canine Science Forum 2027.

The committee will assess proposals based on the following criteria:

Relevance:
Alignment of the proposed research with the aims and scope of the conference
Originality:
Novelty and potential scientific contribution of the research
Clarity:
Clarity of thought, expression, and formulation of the research questions
Research quality:
Scientific rigour, methodological quality, and strength of the research findings
Previous scientific output:
Scientific achievements, publications, and research contributions of the applicant

All achievements and scientific contributions will be evaluated in relation to the applicant's career stage and research opportunities, ensuring fair consideration of both PhD students and postdoctoral researchers.

Results

Notification of Results

  1. The notification date will be announced.

  2. The award recipient will subsequently be publicly announced on the official Canine Science Forum 2027 website.
